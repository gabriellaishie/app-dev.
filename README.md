# My Favorite Korean Drama: Doctor Slump 🩺

![Doctor Slump](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/7/7c/Doctor_Slump_poster.jpg)

## About the Series

**Doctor Slump** is a South Korean romantic comedy television series released in 2024. It stars **Park Hyung-sik** as Yeo Jeong-woo and **Park Shin-hye** as Nam Ha-neul.

The story follows two former academic rivals who unexpectedly reunite during difficult periods in their lives. As they face personal struggles, they find comfort, friendship, and love in each other.

## Why I Love This Series

I love *Doctor Slump* because it shows that life does not always go according to our plans. It reminds me that it is okay to experience difficult moments and that we do not always have to face everything alone.

My favorite part is how the two main characters support each other and slowly find happiness despite their struggles.
